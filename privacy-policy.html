Privacy Policy

ShotWeek is designed to keep the health-related records you enter on your device. This policy explains what is stored locally, what limited information is processed for subscriptions, and the choices available to you.

Effective September 1, 2026 Version 1.0 iOS app

1. Scope and who we are

This Privacy Policy applies to the ShotWeek iOS application, its subscription features, and the ShotWeek legal website that links to this policy (collectively, the “Services”). ShotWeek is provided by its independent developer (“ShotWeek,” “we,” “us,” or “our”).

This policy does not apply to third-party websites, applications, healthcare providers, pharmacies, or services that you choose to access from or in connection with ShotWeek. Their own privacy notices govern their practices.

2. Privacy summary

Private by default. Medication records, dose details, daily response entries, notes, weight entries, and reminder preferences that you enter in ShotWeek are stored locally on your device. ShotWeek does not operate an account system or a cloud database for those records.

  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • We do not use your records for advertising or cross-app tracking.
  • We do not currently include a third-party advertising or analytics SDK.
  • Apple processes App Store payments. RevenueCat helps the app determine subscription and entitlement status.
  • You control exports. Information leaves the app through an export or share action only when you initiate it.

3. Information involved in the Services

Information you choose to enter

Depending on how you use the app, you may enter or select:

  • the name or label of a medication, peptide, supplement, or other compound;
  • dose amount, unit, date, time, cadence, dose phase, source, and injection site;
  • daily response ratings such as appetite, nausea, energy, digestion, sleep, headache, or overall feeling;
  • free-text notes associated with a dose or response entry;
  • weight measurements, measurement units, dates, and related notes;
  • reminder preferences, including whether reminders are enabled and selected times; and
  • onboarding selections such as tracking goal, whether a dose has already started, and which response signals you want to monitor.

ShotWeek does not require you to create an account or provide your name, email address, phone number, postal address, or payment card number to use the local tracking features.

Information generated by the app

The app creates technical identifiers and relationships needed to group dose records and daily response entries, calculate day indexes, show history, enforce a limited free starter allowance, and display patterns based on the records stored on your device.

Support communications

If you email us, we receive the email address, message, and attachments you choose to send. We use that information to respond, troubleshoot, protect the Services, and keep a reasonable support record.

4. Local device storage

ShotWeek stores the records and settings described above in app storage on your iPhone, including Apple-provided local preferences storage. The app also uses the iOS Keychain to remember whether the one-time free starter dose or response allowance has been used. Keychain values may remain on a device after the app is deleted and later reinstalled, depending on iOS behavior.

Because the core records are stored locally, we generally cannot view, retrieve, restore, or remotely delete them. Your device passcode, Apple Account security, backups, operating-system protections, and anyone with access to your unlocked device can affect the confidentiality of local records.

Apple may include application data in an iCloud or computer backup depending on your device and backup settings. Apple, not ShotWeek, controls those backup services. Review your iPhone backup settings if you do not want local app data included in a device backup.

5. Notifications and reminders

If you enable reminders, ShotWeek asks iOS for notification permission and schedules local notifications on your device. Notification settings and scheduled reminder times are used to deliver the reminders you request. You can deny permission, change reminder settings in the app, or disable ShotWeek notifications in iOS Settings.

Notifications can appear on a locked screen depending on your iOS settings. Consider choosing a notification preview setting that matches your privacy needs. Reminders are a convenience only and may be delayed, suppressed, or not delivered because of device settings, Focus modes, operating-system behavior, power state, or other circumstances.

6. Purchases, subscriptions, Apple, and RevenueCat

Subscriptions are purchased through Apple’s App Store. Apple processes payment credentials, billing, taxes, refunds, family-sharing eligibility, and the Apple Account associated with the transaction. ShotWeek does not receive your full payment card number.

ShotWeek uses RevenueCat to retrieve available subscription offerings, initiate purchases, restore purchases, and determine whether premium features should be unlocked. When RevenueCat is configured without an account you create, its software generates an anonymous app user identifier. RevenueCat may process information such as:

  • the anonymous RevenueCat app user identifier;
  • the app, platform, app version, operating system, and relevant device or network metadata;
  • product identifiers, purchase and renewal events, entitlement status, expiration date, and trial or introductory-offer status; and
  • diagnostic information needed to provide and secure its subscription infrastructure.

RevenueCat does not receive the medication, dose, response, weight, or note content stored in ShotWeek merely because the subscription SDK is used. RevenueCat processes subscription data under its own Privacy Policy. Apple’s processing is governed by Apple’s applicable privacy and media-services terms.

7. How information is used

Information is used only as reasonably necessary to:

  • save, display, organize, edit, and export the records you create;
  • associate daily responses with the dose record that started the relevant tracking period;
  • calculate countdowns, schedules, adherence views, trends, comparisons, and summaries from your local entries;
  • schedule local reminders you request;
  • provide, restore, validate, and secure subscription access;
  • enforce limited free usage and prevent repeated circumvention of the starter allowance;
  • respond to support messages, diagnose problems, and protect the Services; and
  • comply with law, enforce our terms, or protect users, ShotWeek, Apple, RevenueCat, or others.

8. Sharing and disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or license your health-related records or personal information to data brokers or advertisers. We do not share those records for targeted or cross-context behavioral advertising.

Information may be disclosed only in the following limited circumstances:

  • At your direction. If you export a report or use the iOS share sheet, the information you select is provided to the destination, person, or application you choose. Review the export before sharing it.
  • Subscription providers. Apple and RevenueCat process the purchase and entitlement information described above.
  • Legal and safety reasons. We may disclose information actually available to us if reasonably necessary to comply with law, legal process, or a valid governmental request, or to protect rights, safety, and the integrity of the Services.
  • Business transition. If the app or its assets are transferred, information actually controlled by us may be part of the transaction, subject to applicable law and this policy. Locally stored records remain on your device unless you choose to transfer them.

9. Health-related and sensitive information

The entries you create may reveal health, medication, body-weight, or wellness information and should be treated as sensitive. ShotWeek is a personal organization and tracking tool; it is not a healthcare provider, health plan, pharmacy, medical device, or medical-record system. ShotWeek is not represented as a “covered entity” or “business associate” under the U.S. Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act (HIPAA).

Do not use ShotWeek as the only copy of information you may need for treatment or emergency care. Do not enter another person’s information unless you are authorized to do so and understand the privacy consequences.

10. Retention, correction, and deletion

Local records

Local records remain on your device until you edit or delete them through available app controls, erase the app’s data, or delete the app. Deleting the app normally removes its local app container, but iOS backups and Keychain values may be retained separately under Apple-controlled behavior and settings.

Subscription records

Apple and RevenueCat retain transaction and subscription information according to their legal obligations and retention policies. If you contact us about RevenueCat data, include the anonymous app user identifier shown in any available diagnostic or support screen so we can determine whether a record can be located. We may ask for reasonable verification before acting on a request.

Support emails

Support communications may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to resolve the request, maintain business records, prevent abuse, and meet legal obligations, after which they may be deleted or de-identified.
